This Privacy Policy describes how R4L: RUN FOR LIFE (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android mobile game available on Google Play. The App is designed to be offline-first: core gameplay and campaign progress work without creating an account.

Summary: Game progress is stored on your device by default. If you use optional features such as advertising, in-app purchases, or future online services, additional data may be processed by us or by third parties (for example Google) as described below.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by the developer reachable at decentralisedpayments@gmail.com. For privacy requests, use this email and include “R4L Privacy” in the subject line.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information stored on your device

The App stores gameplay-related data locally on your device, which may include:

  • Progress (levels completed, stars, unlocks)
  • Virtual currency and upgrade state
  • Settings (audio, haptics, notifications preference)
  • Challenge and achievement status
  • Purchase entitlement flags stored by the App (for example “remove ads”)

This local data is used to run the game and is not required to be uploaded to our servers for basic play. Uninstalling the App or clearing app storage typically deletes this local data.

2.2 Google Play and in-app purchases

If you make purchases through Google Play Billing, Google processes payment and purchase information under Google’s terms and privacy policy. We may receive purchase tokens or product identifiers necessary to unlock features (for example removing ads) and to restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment card details.

2.3 Advertising

The free version of the App may display ads (including rewarded ads, interstitials, and banners) through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) or similar providers. Advertising partners may collect or process identifiers and device/usage data such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address and general location derived from IP
  • Device and app information
  • Ad interaction data

This data is used to deliver, measure, and improve ads. You may reset or limit your advertising ID in Android system settings. If you purchase a “remove ads” product where offered, ads that are covered by that product will no longer be shown; rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a benefit may still be available depending on product design.

2.4 Analytics and diagnostics

We may use analytics or crash-reporting tools to understand stability and feature usage (for example session events, level start/complete, technical error logs). These services may process device and usage information. Where used, they operate under their own privacy terms.

2.5 Optional online features

If the App later enables optional online features (for example cloud save, leaderboards, or signed-in profiles), additional data such as a display name, scores, and account identifiers may be processed to provide those features. Where those features are not enabled, this section does not apply to basic offline play.

2.6 Information you send us directly

If you email us, we process the content of your message and your email address to respond to support or privacy requests.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and maintain gameplay and progress
  • To process purchases and entitlements
  • To show ads in the free version and measure ad performance
  • To improve stability, balance, and user experience
  • To respond to support and legal requests

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your region, we process information as needed to provide the App (contract/performance), with your consent where required (for example certain advertising or analytics), and for legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, and product improvement, balanced against your rights.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers — hosting, analytics, advertising, and payment infrastructure (including Google)
  • Legal authorities — when required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Business transfers — if the App or related assets are transferred, data may transfer under appropriate protections

6. Data retention

  • On-device save data — retained until you delete the App, clear storage, or reset progress
  • Support emails — retained as long as needed to handle your request and related records
  • Ads / analytics / Play — retained according to those providers’ policies

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience emergency-response style game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

If you use the App with a Google Play account subject to family or supervised settings, Google’s policies for that account also apply.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to a mobile game to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Please keep your device and Google account secured.

9. International processing

Third-party providers (including Google) may process data in countries other than your own. Those providers apply their own safeguards and terms.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing, or to object or withdraw consent. You can:

  • Delete local App data via Android App info → Storage
  • Manage advertising preferences and Advertising ID in Android settings
  • Manage purchases and Google account settings in Google Play
  • Contact us at decentralisedpayments@gmail.com for privacy requests

The App may use third-party services such as:
