Request account deletion.

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated personal data for Block Blow: Block Puzzle, published by Msito Studio. You can contact our support team directly using the button below.

How to request deletion

Please send your request from an email address you can access and include enough information for us to identify the account.

  1. Tap Request account deletion below.
  2. In the email, provide your in-game username or player ID, if available.
  3. Send the email. We may contact you if we need to verify the request.
✉ Request account deletion msitostudio@gmail.com
Sending this email starts a deletion request; it does not instantly delete your data. Please do not include passwords, private keys, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

What happens next?

What data will be deleted?

Msito Studio will review your request and delete the account-associated personal data that can be deleted, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and record-keeping requirements. Any data that must be retained will be limited to what is required and kept only for the required period.

How long does deletion take?

We will review your request and respond by email. The time needed to complete deletion may depend on identity verification and any applicable retention requirements. If your team has a defined deletion timeframe, update this page to state it clearly before publishing.

Can I request deletion if I cannot access the game?

Yes. Email msitostudio@gmail.com and provide your in-game username or player ID if you know it. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the account is yours.
