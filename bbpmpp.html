Depending on how you use the Game, we may collect the following:

  • Display name / player name — if you set or change a name for leaderboards or profile features.
  • Optional communications — if you email us for support, we receive the content of your message and your email address.

  • Advertising identifiers — where allowed by platform settings (for example advertising ID on Android or IDFA-related signals on iOS when permitted), used for ads measurement and personalization subject to your device choices.
  • Purchase-related data — product identifiers and transaction status for in-app purchases (e.g. remove ads, premium, or power-up packs). Payment card details are processed by the app store (Apple/Google), not by us.

2.3 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not require you to create an email-and-password account to play. We do not knowingly ask for government ID, precise continuous GPS tracking for gameplay, or contacts from your address book.
