This Privacy Policy describes how MSITO STUDIO (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the mobile application Stuck Tile (the “App”), available on Google Play and other platforms where we publish it.

Stuck Tile is a casual offline-first puzzle game with three modes—Stuck, Stack, and Match— featuring local progress, optional advertisements, and optional in-app purchases.

Short summary: Gameplay progress is stored on your device. We do not require an account. If ads are shown, Google AdMob and related partners may collect device and advertising data under their policies. Purchases are processed by Google Play. You can remove ads via an in-app purchase where offered.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to users of the Stuck Tile App. The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner that violates applicable law (including the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, where applicable). See Section 10.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information stored on your device (local)

The App stores data locally on your device to provide core features. This typically includes:

  • Game progress (completed levels, stars, current world/level)
  • Virtual currency and consumables used in the App (for example coins and hints)
  • Settings preferences (sound, music, haptics)
  • Entitlements such as “Remove Ads” and owned cosmetic themes, when purchased or restored
  • Other local game state needed for offline play

This local data is used to operate the App and is not uploaded to our servers as part of standard gameplay. Uninstalling the App or clearing App data may delete this information.

2.2 Information we do not require

  • We do not require you to create an account to play
  • We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to access core gameplay
  • We do not request access to your contacts, photos, or precise location for gameplay

2.3 Advertising-related data (when ads are enabled)

If you have not purchased or restored a “Remove Ads” entitlement, the App may show advertisements provided by Google AdMob and, where used, mediation partners. These services may collect or process data such as:

  • Device identifiers (for example advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Device type, OS version, app version, and language
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, rewarded ad completion)
  • Diagnostic information related to ad delivery

Ad serving may use cookies or similar technologies on some platforms as described by the ad provider. For details, see Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob help.

2.4 In-app purchases

Optional purchases (for example Remove Ads and cosmetic themes) are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google may provide us with purchase tokens or product identifiers needed to unlock and restore entitlements. Payment data is handled under Google’s terms and privacy policy.

2.5 Diagnostics and analytics (if enabled)

The App may use aggregated or pseudonymous diagnostics (for example crash reports or performance metrics) to improve stability. If a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK is enabled in a given build, that provider’s privacy policy also applies. We aim to limit such data to what is needed to maintain the App.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve gameplay and offline progress
  • Remember settings and purchased entitlements
  • Show ads where permitted and measure basic ad performance
  • Process and restore in-app purchases
  • Detect abuse, fraud, or technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract - providing the App and features you request
  • Legitimate interests - securing the App, understanding aggregate usage, improving reliability
  • Consent - where required for personalized advertising or non-essential cookies/SDKs (you may withdraw consent via device settings or in-App prompts where shown)
  • Legal obligation - when the law requires processing

5. Advertising, consent, and your choices

  • Remove Ads: Where offered, purchasing “Remove Ads” disables banner and interstitial ads in the App. Rewarded ads may still be available as an optional way to earn in-game rewards, or may be adjusted as described in the App.
  • Opt out of personalized ads (Android): You can reset or limit your advertising ID in device settings (for example Google settings → Ads).
  • Consent forms: In regions that require it (for example EEA/UK), the App may present a consent message (such as Google’s UMP consent form) before personalized ads.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers that help operate the App (for example ad networks, cloud or crash tools if enabled)
  • Platform operators such as Google Play for distribution, payments, and policy compliance
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

7. Data retention

  • Local game data: retained on your device until you clear App data or uninstall
  • Purchase entitlements: associated records may be retained as needed to restore purchases and prevent fraud
  • Ad/analytics data: retained by third parties according to their policies

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a consumer mobile game. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for controlling access to your device.

9. International transfers

If you use the App outside the country where our operator is based, information processed by third parties (for example Google) may be processed in other countries. Those providers are responsible for applying appropriate safeguards as described in their policies.

10. Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) for the purpose of selling data or enabling unauthorized tracking. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
